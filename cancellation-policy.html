Order Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Operating Facility: 52/2 Malegaon - 423203

1. Cancellation Window (Prior to Dispatch)

You may cancel your spice order at any time prior to parcel dispatch from our packing center (typically within 12 hours of placing your order).

To request cancellation, immediately message us on WhatsApp at +91 7620098478 or email ojaswifoods@gmail.com with your Order ID (#OJ------).

2. Post-Dispatch Status

Once an order has been marked as Shipped and handed over to our national courier partners, the shipment cannot be intercepted or cancelled in transit due to food safety chain custody rules.

3. Cancellation by Ojaswi Foods

Ojaswi Foods reserves the right to cancel any order if:

  • The delivery destination PIN code is completely unserviceable by express courier partners.
  • The contact phone number provided by the customer cannot be verified or is persistently unreachable.
  • Unforeseen raw material harvest shortages prevent us from fulfilling our signature quality standard.

In any such event, the customer will be notified immediately, and 100% of any prepaid amounts will be refunded without deduction.

4. Order Cancellation Assistance

To request cancellation of an order before dispatch, contact our support helpline at +91 7620098478 or email ojaswifoods@gmail.com with your Order ID.
