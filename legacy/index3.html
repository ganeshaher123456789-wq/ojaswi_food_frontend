Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ojaswi stone-ground spices, Malegaon manufacturing, FSSAI licensing, orders, and free home delivery.

What makes Ojaswi Spices authentic and pure? +
Ojaswi Spices are 100% stone-ground in traditional small batches at our dedicated manufacturing unit located at 52/2, Malegaon - 423203, Nashik, Maharashtra. Our cold stone-grinding process preserves the natural essential oils and aromas that high-speed industrial grinding usually burns away. We never add artificial colors, synthetic flavors, chemical preservatives, or MSG.
What is the FSSAI license number of Ojaswi Foods? +
Ojaswi Foods is fully registered and compliant with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India under Central FSSAI License No. 3025120612245599988. Each batch is meticulously tested for moisture content, aroma purity, and cleanliness before packing.
What is the shelf life and proper storage method for Ojaswi spice packs? +
All our spice packs have a shelf life of 12 months from the date of packaging. Once opened, transfer the spices into an airtight glass or stainless-steel container and store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and stove heat to maintain peak fragrance and pungency.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD) and Free Home Delivery? +
Yes! We provide 100% Free Shipping across all pin codes in India on every spice order with no minimum order value. You can pay securely online via UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking, or choose Cash on Delivery (COD) to pay directly upon courier delivery at your doorstep.
