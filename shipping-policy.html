Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Dispatch Origin: Ojaswi Foods, 52/2 Malegaon - 423203

1. Shipping Coverage & Delivery Charges

We deliver authentic Ojaswi Premium spice blends across major PIN codes in India.

  • Standard Delivery Charges: FREE SHIPPING on all premium spice orders across India.
  • Packaging: Multi-layer moisture-proof, aroma-sealed protective pouches and corrugated boxes to ensure maximum freshness during transport.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch Timeline

Every spice order is packed fresh at our Malegaon facility:

  • Processing Time: Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on working days are processed and packed within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Delivery Duration (Maharashtra): 2 to 4 business days.
  • Delivery Duration (Rest of India): 4 to 7 business days depending on destination PIN code and local logistics.

3. Shipment Tracking

Once your order is packed and dispatched, customers receive automated updates via SMS and WhatsApp. You can also view live tracking anytime directly at ojaswifoods.com/orders using your Order ID (#OJ------).

4. Delivery Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate delivery addresses including House number, Area/Street, Landmark, and 6-digit Indian PIN code. Courier partners will make up to three attempts to deliver the parcel.

5. Shipping Queries & Support

For questions regarding dispatch status or delay inquiries, contact our logistics desk at +91 7620098478 or email ojaswifoods@gmail.com.
