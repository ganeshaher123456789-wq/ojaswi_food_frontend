Refund & Return Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Food Safety Standard for Ojaswi Foods

1. Food Consumables Policy

In strict compliance with Indian food safety standards and FSSAI hygiene regulations, packaged consumable food items like spices and blended masalas cannot be returned once the protective outer packaging seal has been unsealed or tampered with.

2. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

We take immense care in packing every spice order. However, if your order arrives in any of the following conditions:

  • The parcel package was visibly damaged or crushed in courier transit.
  • The inner spice pouch seal was broken or punctured upon delivery.
  • An incorrect spice product was delivered compared to your order confirmation receipt.

Resolution: We will provide a 100% free immediate replacement pack or full refund.

3. Claim Process (Within 48 Hours)

To request a replacement or refund:

  1. Contact our Malegaon support team within 48 hours of parcel delivery via WhatsApp at +91 7972483489 or email ojaswifoods@gmail.com.
  2. Provide your Order ID (e.g. #OJ------) and clear photos showing the damaged package or outer shipping label.
  3. Our team will inspect and approve the claim within 24 business hours.

4. Refund Timeline & Method

All orders on Ojaswi Premium are processed via Cash on Delivery (COD). In the rare event a replacement or refund is approved, refunds are sent directly to your bank account via UPI or NEFT within 24 to 48 hours of verification.
